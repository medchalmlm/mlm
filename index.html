📜 Society Rules & Bylaws

🚶 Visitor Policy
  • All visitors must register at the gate
  • Maids, helpers & construction workers must have a valid entry pass
  • No unapproved commercial activity
🔇 Noise & Timings
  • No loud music/TV after 10:00 PM
  • Drilling/construction: 9 AM – 6 PM only
  • Party/event: inform committee 48 hrs prior
🐶 Pet Policy
  • Keep pets on leash in all common areas
  • Parks are pet-free zones
  • Always carry a disposal bag and clean up promptly
  • Keep pet vaccinations current and valid
🗑️ Garbage Disposal
  • Municipal van collects waste daily — please hand over on time
  • No dumping in parks, roads or common areas
  • Let's keep our society clean and beautiful 🌿
